## Purpose

Payment milestones express when and how much the business expects to invoice.
They remain core because they can belong directly to a Funnel, even when the
Projects plugin is disabled.

## Setting up milestones

1. Open the Funnel’s primary quotation and select **Payment Milestones**. You can prepare a plan before Commit.
2. Start from the quotation items. Split an item when it will be billed in more than one period. For each row enter its milestone title, amount excluding tax, expected invoice month and year. Expand a row to review details or enter remarks.
3. Check **Remaining**. For example, split a RM 10,000 net item into RM 4,000 in November and RM 6,000 in December. The complete plan must reconcile to the quotation net in its own currency. Save the milestones.
4. Open the Funnel’s **Payment Milestones** tab to review the rows alongside their source quotation and item. An older Funnel-level row can have no source quotation. Open the milestone to review its details.

## Milestone statuses

**Pending invoicing** is a planned billing event. **Invoiced** records that billing took place. The Funnel stage and the milestone status are separate.

## Managing milestones

After billing, an authorized user opens the eligible milestone and selects **Mark invoiced**. This records planning progress. Issue invoices and record receipts in Billing. Invoiced or financially linked rows retain their financial history; permitted forecast period corrections preserve the original amount.

## Boundary rule

Milestones describe expected billing. Invoices, receipts, purchase invoices,
and payments are Finance documents and must not be added to this table.
